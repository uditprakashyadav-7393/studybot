## 🎯 About StudyBot

StudyBot is an intelligent chatbot designed specifically for students in Classes 9-12, providing instant academic support, doubt resolution, and exam preparation guidance. Always available, always ready to help you excel in your studies!

Whether you're struggling with a concept, preparing for exams, or need quick clarification on a topic, StudyBot is your personal AI tutor.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 📖 **Study Topics**
- **Class-Specific Content:** Access topics curated for your class level (9, 10, 11, or 12)
- **One-Click Learning:** Browse topics and instantly send prompts to StudyBot with a single click
- **Comprehensive Coverage:** Topics span multiple subjects including Science, Mathematics, English, Social Studies, and more

### 💬 **24/7 Doubt Resolution**
- Get instant answers to your academic questions
- Clear explanations for difficult concepts
- Real-time chat support anytime, anywhere

### 🏆 **Exam Preparation**
- Targeted exam prep guides for board exams and competitive tests
- Practice question prompts
- Tips and strategies for scoring better
- Topic-wise revision assistance

### 🎓 **Smart Learning**
- AI-powered responses tailored to your class level
- Easy-to-understand explanations
- Topic suggestions based on curriculum

---

## 🚀 How to Use

1. **Visit StudyBot:** Go to [Study Bot](https://studybot.great-site.net)
2. **Select Your Class:** Choose your class (9, 10, 11, or 12)
3. **Browse Study Topics:** View topics organized by subject
4. **Send Prompt:** Click on a topic to send a pre-formatted prompt to the chatbot
5. **Get Answers:** Receive detailed, student-friendly explanations
